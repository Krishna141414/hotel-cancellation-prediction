What makes a booking likely to cancel?

Three views that agree with each other: permutation importance of the final model (how much the test score drops when one input is shuffled), the trees' own impurity importance, and the readable top of a decision tree.

Permutation importance

Drop in test score when an input is shuffled (mean ± std over repeats).

In plain English

Cancellation rates in the training data for the top drivers.

Tree-model importance

Impurity-based, summed per input column (training fit).

Largest weights on scaled inputs: pink raises the odds of cancelling, teal lowers them.
